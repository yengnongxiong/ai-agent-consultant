# Workforce readiness: first draft

> Fictional company, written to demo Second Cut. Any resemblance to a real business is coincidental.

**Company:** Corvane Industrial Supply. 1,150 employees across 38 branches, a central
inside-sales team of 35, and a four-person IT department.

## Point of view

Corvane's workforce is loyal and experienced but built for counter and phone sales. Any move
to digital ordering has to be paced to what the organization can absorb this year.

## Constraints leadership has already set

- **A hiring freeze holds through the end of next fiscal year.** Headcount stays flat, and the
  inside-sales team will shrink from 35 to 28 through attrition.
- **IT is fully committed to the ERP upgrade until Q3.** The upgrade migrates 12 years of order
  history into a new data model, and the team has flagged duplicate and mislabeled product
  codes in roughly a fifth of historical orders.

## What we heard from the field

- Branch staff average 14 years of tenure. In a recent internal survey, 60% said they have
  never used the e-commerce admin tools.
- Field and inside-sales reps are paid commission only on orders they personally place.
  Orders that customers place online earn reps nothing.
- In last year's customer survey, 41 of 50 contractors said they prefer to order by phone or
  at the counter, mostly because they rely on branch staff to catch mistakes in their orders.

## Recommended moves

1. Redesign rep compensation before launching any digital channel, so reps are rewarded for
   accounts they grow, not only orders they key in.
2. Pilot digital ordering at three branches with the most tech-comfortable staff.
3. Train branch staff on the e-commerce tools before customers are invited to use them.

## Next steps

- Draft a compensation proposal for review next quarter.
- Pick pilot branches with input from regional managers.
